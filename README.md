FullStack developer in training, with a solid foundation in frontend (JS, CSS, React). I work with Node.js, Express, and PostgreSQL, and in my free time I explore game development and mods for Minecraft Java.

## 👋 About Me
I'm a **FullStack** developer, focused on **Backend**, though I'm comfortable working in frontend as well (**HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, React**). I'm currently deepening my skills in **NodeJs**, **ExpressJs**, and **PostgreSQL**, building projects that integrate both sides of the stack.

Outside of web development, I enjoy programming Minecraft Mods in NeoForge with **Java**, and experimenting with game development in **Unity** — it's where I combine my love of programming with creativity.
